Hi, welcome to my website! I am Atreya Vedantam, a final-year student in the five-year Dual Degree (B.Tech. + M.Tech.) program in [Electrical Engineering at IIT Madras](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/).

I am interested in the foundations of modern machine learning. Broadly I ask the question:

*How do the mechanisms and constraints of learning shape the representations, behavior, and generalization of modern models?*

This question motivates my research at the intersection of machine learning, optimization, and statistics. More concretely, I am interested in how optimization shapes learned representations, and how constraints such as limited memory and privacy change what can be learned. I am especially drawn to problems where theory explains an observed phenomenon and suggests a better algorithm. You can find my work, grouped by topic, on the [Publications](/publications/) page.

Currently, I am working with [Prof. Vatsal Sharan](https://vatsalsharan.github.io/) on how the choice of optimizer shapes feature learning, continuing my summer internship at the University of Southern California as an IUSSTF–Viterbi Scholar. My master's thesis, advised by Vatsal Sharan, [Kabir Verchand](https://kabirverchand.github.io/) (USC) and [Ankit Pensia](https://ankitp.net/) (CMU), is on memory-sample tradeoffs in high-dimensional statistics. At IIT Madras, I am fortunate to work with [Prof. Krishna Pillutla](https://krishnap25.github.io/) on privacy-utility tradeoffs in fine-tuning, and I have worked with Prof. Chandrashekar Lakshminarayanan on delayed generalization in linear models.

In the past I have worked in different areas. I am grateful to have worked with [Prof. Krishna Jagannathan](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/~krishnaj/) for my B.Tech. Project (BTP) thesis on stochastic modeling (entanglement scheduling in quantum networks), [Prof. Radha Krishna Ganti](https://www.rkganti.in/) on algebraic coding theory (code rate recovery in linear block codes) and [Prof. Deepa Venkitesh](https://sites.google.com/a/ee.iitm.ac.in/deepav/) and [Prof. Lakshmi Narasimhan Theagarajan](https://sites.google.com/view/lakshminarasimhan/bio) on spectral analysis (real-time spectrograms with dispersive fibres).

In what seems like a previous lifetime, I was interested in quantitative finance. I enjoyed spending a summer interning at [Dynamic Technology Lab](https://www.dytechlab.com/), a hedge fund based in Singapore.

In my free time, I play tennis and sing Carnatic (Indian classical) music. I also enjoy reading fiction, and I [write](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/18595417.Atreya_Vedantam) too.

**I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.**
